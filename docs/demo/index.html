民国书衣

luxun-cover-skill

给人和编程代理用的一套书衣工具。几何纹样、暖纸、墨加一个强调色、大块余白，书名与纹样分开。

An agent skill and a local Python generator for Republican-era Chinese book covers: geometric ornament, warm paper, ink plus one accent, and wide margins. The title stays apart from the ornament.

这些是致敬构图，不是原封面的复制，也不是陶元庆原画的临摹。《呐喊》初版封面是鲁迅自己做的，《野草》封面是孙福熙做的。这里只用这些书名，版式是新的。几何纹样在程序里算出来，SVG 的说明里留着 not a facsimile。

Homage compositions, not facsimiles of the original covers, and not tracings of Tao Yuanqing’s paintings. The first-edition cover of 《呐喊》 was designed by Lu Xun; the cover of 《野草》 was designed by Sun Fuxi. These layouts are new. The ornaments are computed, and the SVG keeps the words “not a facsimile”.

生成一张

Install, then make one cover.

pip install -e .
luxun-cover --title 彷徨 --out panghuang.svg
